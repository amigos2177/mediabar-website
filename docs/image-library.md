## Selection rules

- Use production photography when explaining process or capability.
- Use final stills when explaining the quality or emotional value of the finished work.
- Use team photography for About, leadership, trust, and culture sections.
- Do not use one photograph more than once on the same page.
- Before reusing a photograph across pages, confirm that a more specific unused asset
  is not available in this catalog.
- Keep alt text factual and describe what is visible. Do not infer a client, event,
  person, or location that is not clearly verified.
