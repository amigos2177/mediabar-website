Our Story

BUILT IN
SAN ANTONIO.
For San Antonio.

"Your Vision Served Daily."

- Media Bar Productions, Founder

Media Bar Productions was founded in 2011 with a simple belief: San Antonio businesses deserved access to the same level of video production quality that national brands in major markets took for granted. Not overpriced agency work. Not cut-rate production that made companies look small. Real broadcast-quality video, produced by a team that genuinely cared about the outcome.

What started as a focused video production operation has grown into one of San Antonio's most decorated production companies - 3 Emmy Awards, 15 Telly Awards, and a client list that includes the San Antonio Spurs, HEB, Unilever, Frost Bank, and organizations across Texas who trusted us to represent their brand on screen.

We're still based in San Antonio. We still believe the work speaks for itself. And we still approach every project - from a 30-second social media clip to a full national commercial - with the same level of commitment we brought to our very first shoot.

How We Work

WHAT WE
Believe In

🎯
The Work First

Every decision we make - camera, lighting, edit, music - is in service of making the work better. Not faster. Not cheaper. Better. The clients who work with us long-term understand that distinction.

🤝
Your Footage Belongs to You

We believe the footage we film for you belongs to you - not us. Every raw file from every project is delivered to you at close. No licensing, no storage fees, no conditions. It's yours.

📞
We're Always Available

You should be able to call or email at any stage of a project and get a real answer from a real person. No runaround, no waiting for a project manager to relay a message. Direct access, always.

💡
Honest Pricing

We quote projects clearly and completely upfront. No hidden fees, no scope creep surprises, no billing for things we didn't agree to. What we quote is what you pay - unless you change the scope.

🏆
Broadcast Standards, Every Time

Three Emmy Awards aren't something you win by doing work that's "pretty good." The standard we hold ourselves to on every project is the same one that earns broadcast industry recognition.

🌆
San Antonio Is Home

We've been part of this city since 2011. We've watched it grow, worked with the businesses that define it, and we're deeply invested in its continued success. This isn't just where we work - it's home.
